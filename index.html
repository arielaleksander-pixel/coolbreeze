About Cool Breeze

Cool Breeze Heating & Air Conditioning

A 100% women-owned HVAC company based in Reisterstown, serving homes across Central Maryland. The team takes a service-first approach: look at each home and recommend what it actually needs, with clear answers and honest recommendations, without confusion or pressure.

Besides service and replacement work, residential new construction is a major specialty: Cool Breeze plans and installs HVAC for builders, from load calculations and custom ductwork through rough-in, equipment installation, startup and final walkthrough.
